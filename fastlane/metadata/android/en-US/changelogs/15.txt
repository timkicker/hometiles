Widgets that open their own setup screen before they are placed, like a door entry or a mail widget, got stuck in the picker and never reached the tile. They land on it now (issue 9). Holding a set speed dial key still calls straight away, but the line above the keypad now says so and a tap on it opens the settings right at the speed dial, where a key can be cleared and set again. That settings row is called Calls and speed dial now (issue 10).
