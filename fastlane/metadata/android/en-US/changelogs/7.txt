A ringing call now opens the call screen even while the phone is in use. Its notice can answer or reject. During a call the number keys send tones, star and pound included. New is the setting Phone with keys, which starts lists on the first entry and leaves out photos, something that suits flip phones in particular. Speed dials can be cleared in the settings. US numbers group as you type, borders stay visible in the light theme and voicemail counts on the phone tile.
